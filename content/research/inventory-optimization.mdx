## Decision problem

The model balances holding cost, shortage penalties, and service targets under uncertain and potentially shifting demand.

## Evaluation

Candidate policies are calibrated on historical demand and evaluated across Monte Carlo scenarios. Stress tests alter volatility, lead time, seasonality, and cost assumptions to identify policies that remain defensible outside one estimated regime.

## Main finding

Robust parameter choices can dominate narrowly optimized rules once demand shifts, making sensitivity analysis central to the decision rather than a final appendix.
