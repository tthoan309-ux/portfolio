## Research focus

This project compares interpretable linear classification with tree-based ensemble methods. The objective is not only to identify likely churn, but also to understand which customer signals can support targeted intervention.

## Analytical approach

The workflow covers preprocessing, validation, threshold selection, and comparative evaluation. Results are interpreted in the context of operational retention decisions.
