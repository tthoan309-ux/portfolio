## Research question

Which customer behaviors signal attrition, and how should a retention team choose a decision threshold when intervention costs are not symmetric?

## Comparative design

The analysis compares logistic regression with tree-based ensembles under consistent validation splits. Model quality is assessed through ranking, calibration, class-sensitive error, and the operational consequences of alternative thresholds.

## Main finding

Ensemble methods improve predictive ranking, while the linear baseline provides a stable account of the behavioral signals associated with churn. The preferred system combines both perspectives instead of treating the highest score as the only result.
