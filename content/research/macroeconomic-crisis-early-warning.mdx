## Research question

Can a predictive system identify macroeconomic vulnerability before crisis conditions become fully visible, while remaining interpretable enough for policy monitoring?

## System design

The project frames early warning as a rare-event problem. It compares linear and nonlinear classifiers, calibrates predicted risks, evaluates class-sensitive metrics, and examines the stability of warnings across validation windows.

## Explainability

SHAP analysis is used at two levels: to identify global vulnerability patterns and to inspect the indicator configuration behind an individual warning. This makes model disagreement and unexpected alerts available for scrutiny.

## Main finding

Nonlinear models capture interactions among external, fiscal, and financial indicators that simpler specifications miss. Calibration and explanation remain necessary before predictive gains can become credible decision support.
