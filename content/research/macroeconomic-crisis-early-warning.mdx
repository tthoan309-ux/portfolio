## Research focus

The project frames crisis detection as an imbalanced classification problem. It evaluates whether nonlinear models can capture interactions among macroeconomic indicators while remaining useful to analysts and policymakers.

## Analytical approach

Model performance is paired with explainability. SHAP values are used to inspect global drivers and individual risk signals, helping distinguish a high-performing alert from a policy-relevant one.
