## Research focus

The dashboard turns operational records into a coherent decision surface. It prioritizes clear comparisons, drill-down paths, and indicators that can reveal where delays and performance differences concentrate.

## Analytical approach

Measures and views are designed around operational questions rather than decorative charts. The resulting dashboard supports both high-level monitoring and route-level investigation.
