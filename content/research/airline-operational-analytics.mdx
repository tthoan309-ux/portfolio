## Decision problem

Where do operational delays concentrate, and which route, time, and service dimensions provide the clearest intervention points?

## Analytical design

The interface is organized around progressive investigation: network performance, temporal patterns, route comparisons, and record-level detail. Measures use consistent operational definitions so that users can move between views without changing the meaning of a KPI.

## Main finding

The analysis surfaces recurring concentrations of delay and meaningful service-level variation that are difficult to see in aggregate reports. The contribution is the decision path—not the dashboard software itself.
