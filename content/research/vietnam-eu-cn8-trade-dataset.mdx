## Data problem

Monthly product-level trade records change across releases, classifications, and reporting conventions. Without explicit reconciliation, these breaks can look like genuine economic effects.

## Pipeline design

Automated acquisition is followed by schema tests, product-code concordance, partner normalization, duplicate checks, unit reconciliation, and coverage reports. Releases are versioned so that a result can be reproduced against the exact data state used in analysis.

## Research value

The dataset is designed as infrastructure for event studies, trade-policy evaluation, product survival analysis, and research on Vietnam's integration with European markets.
