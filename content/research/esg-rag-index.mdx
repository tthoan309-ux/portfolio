## Infrastructure problem

ESG reports are lengthy, heterogeneous, and difficult to compare. A useful research index must retrieve relevant evidence without separating a claim from its source context.

## Pipeline design

The system ingests documents, preserves page structure, attaches issuer and reporting metadata, and versions every extraction. Retrieval tests use question sets and citation checks rather than relying on anecdotal outputs.

## Contribution

Every result is designed to be traceable to a source document, page, reporting period, and pipeline version. This turns retrieval infrastructure into an auditable component of ESG research.
