## Research question

Can graph representations recover economically meaningful similarity among countries, products, or firms and improve downstream analysis without obscuring why actors are considered similar?

## Evaluation design

Embeddings are compared with network statistics and simple matrix baselines on link prediction and clustering tasks. Neighborhood inspection tests whether proximity corresponds to recognizable production or trade relationships.

## Research stance

The project treats a low-dimensional visualization as exploratory evidence, not validation. Representations must demonstrate value on explicit economic tasks and remain open to substantive interpretation.
