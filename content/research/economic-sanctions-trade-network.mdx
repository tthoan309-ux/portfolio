## Research question

How do sanctions alter trade connectivity, partner substitution, and the architecture of international production networks?

## Contribution

The study connects conventional trade-flow evidence with network topology. This distinguishes a decline in direct exchange from a deeper reorganization in which trade is rerouted through new partners or communities.

## Evaluation

Network measures are tracked around sanctions events and interpreted alongside product-level heterogeneity. Placebo periods and alternative network thresholds test whether detected reconfiguration is specific to the shock definition.
