## Optimization problem

The problem assigns customer deliveries to vehicle routes while respecting capacity, timing, and service constraints and minimizing total operating cost.

## Algorithm

Adaptive Large Neighborhood Search alternates destroy and repair operators, learning which combinations perform well for the current instance. Acceptance rules preserve exploration while the search records operator contribution and convergence behavior.

## Evaluation

Solution quality, runtime, stability, and constraint violations are compared with constructive and fixed-neighborhood baselines. Sensitivity analysis tests whether gains persist as demand and time-window pressure change.
