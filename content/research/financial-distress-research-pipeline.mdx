## Research system

This is not only a classification model. It is an end-to-end empirical pipeline designed to make public financial disclosures usable for reproducible distress research.

## Data acquisition and normalization

Automated CafeF crawlers collect issuer pages, financial tables, and linked reports. Structured tables are parsed directly; scanned or irregular PDFs pass through OCR and layout-aware extraction. A normalization layer reconciles labels, units, periods, and accounting identities before records enter the analytical dataset.

## Modeling and evaluation

Feature engineering encodes liquidity, leverage, profitability, cash-flow, and trajectory signals. Models are evaluated with temporal splits, rare-event metrics, calibration, and leakage checks. SHAP explanations are stored alongside predictions so that both global drivers and company-specific alerts can be reviewed.

## Contribution

The principal contribution is an auditable chain from source document to model result. Extraction confidence, reconciliation decisions, dataset versions, and evaluation outputs are preserved as research artifacts.
