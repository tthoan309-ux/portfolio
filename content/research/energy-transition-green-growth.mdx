## Research question

How does energy transition influence green growth over time, and when does public attention strengthen or weaken that relationship?

## Current direction

The working paper develops a dynamic empirical framework that treats public attention as an economically meaningful moderating variable rather than background context.

## Research status

The literature review and data-collection stages are active. Model development will follow the final measurement design; findings are intentionally not reported before validation.
