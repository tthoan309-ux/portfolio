## Research question

How does energy transition influence green growth over time, and when does public attention strengthen or weaken that relationship?

## Current direction

The working paper develops a dynamic empirical framework that treats public attention as an economically meaningful moderating variable rather than background context. The research remains in progress and findings will be added after validation.
