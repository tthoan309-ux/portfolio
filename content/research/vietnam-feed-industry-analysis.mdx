## Research question

How do imported input dependence and market concentration shape cost exposure in Vietnam's animal-feed industry?

## Contribution

The project integrates evidence that is usually studied separately: import composition, global commodity prices, firm structure, and domestic production. The resulting industry map supports a more precise account of vulnerability than sector totals alone.

## Research status

Data harmonization and industry classification are in progress. Findings remain provisional until firm and trade records are reconciled.
