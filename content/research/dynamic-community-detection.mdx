## Research question

When do detected trade communities reflect durable economic structure rather than short-lived variation in trade flows?

## Computational approach

The study constructs comparable network layers across time, detects communities under alternative resolution settings, and tracks membership changes around economic events. Stability metrics are interpreted alongside substantive trade relationships.

## Contribution

The framework makes temporal persistence an explicit evaluation criterion, avoiding the common mistake of treating each year's partition as unrelated.
