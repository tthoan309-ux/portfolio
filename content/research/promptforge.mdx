## Reproducibility problem

Prompt-based workflows often change without a corresponding record of what changed, which examples were tested, or how outputs were judged. This creates hidden methodological drift.

## System design

PromptForge stores prompt versions, model settings, input fixtures, output schemas, and evaluation criteria as one experiment definition. Comparative runs produce structured traces suitable for review and regression testing.

## Contribution

The framework treats prompt engineering as experimental research software rather than informal trial and error.
