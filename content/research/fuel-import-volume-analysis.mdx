## Research question

Which macroeconomic conditions explain changes in fuel import volume after accounting for persistent differences across markets?

## Analytical contribution

The study treats panel structure as part of the economic question rather than a technical afterthought. Alternative specifications distinguish changes occurring within markets from persistent differences between them.

## Research design

The workflow covers data harmonization, specification selection, diagnostic testing, and robustness analysis. Results are interpreted as conditional associations, with explicit attention to model assumptions and the limits of the available data.

## Main finding

Estimated demand relationships remain stable across the preferred specifications, while tests show that ignoring unobserved market heterogeneity would produce a misleading account of import behavior.
