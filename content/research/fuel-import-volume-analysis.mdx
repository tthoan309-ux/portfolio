## Research focus

This project examines variation in fuel import volumes through an applied-economics lens. The empirical workflow emphasizes panel-data structure, transparent model specification, and diagnostic testing.

## Analytical approach

The analysis is organized around interpretable econometric estimates rather than prediction alone. Alternative specifications are compared to understand the stability of estimated relationships and the role of observed heterogeneity.
