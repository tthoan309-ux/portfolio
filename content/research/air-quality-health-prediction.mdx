## Research question

How reliably can environmental exposure indicators predict health-related outcomes, and which signals remain informative outside the estimation sample?

## Research design

Regression specifications are evaluated through out-of-sample error, residual diagnostics, and feature stability. The study separates predictive evidence from causal claims and documents the observational limitations of the dataset.

## Main finding

A compact set of exposure measures contributes consistently to prediction, but the analysis also shows why forecast performance alone cannot establish a causal health effect.
