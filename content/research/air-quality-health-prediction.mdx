## Research focus

This project studies how environmental exposure data can be modeled alongside health outcomes. It combines predictive performance with attention to feature interpretation and the limits of observational evidence.

## Analytical approach

The workflow compares regression specifications, evaluates out-of-sample error, and examines which environmental indicators contribute most consistently to predictions.
