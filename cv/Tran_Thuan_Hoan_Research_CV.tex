\section{Selected Research Projects}
\case{Fragmented Globalization and Vietnam's Automotive Value Chains}{Completed research paper}{A 2015--2023 political-economy study finds that Vietnam expanded automotive production and exports while domestic value capture and localization remained limited. Supplier diversification did not remove dependence on established East Asian production ecosystems.}

\case{Geopolitical Risk, Supplier Concentration, and Fuel Imports}{Completed econometric study}{A 714-observation country panel for 2001--2021 estimates an inverted-U conditional association between geopolitical risk and fuel-import volume. Supplier concentration is positively associated with import volume in the preferred fixed-effects specifications.}

\case{Operating Synergy and Profitability}{Completed econometric analysis}{Across the reported OLS, fixed-effects, random-effects, Driscoll--Kraay, and System GMM models, the interaction between gross margin and asset turnover is positive. The result is interpreted as a conditional association rather than a causal effect.}

\case{Carbon Intensity, Debt Concern, and Green Growth}{Completed empirical report}{The baseline country fixed-effects model reports negative coefficients for carbon intensity, debt concern, and their interaction. IV and dynamic specifications do not reproduce the interaction, so the conclusion remains limited to the baseline association.}

\section{Selected Empirical Evidence - Continued}
\case{Trade Reallocation Without Aggregate Diversion}{Completed empirical report}{A 415,631 country-pair-year structural-gravity panel associates sanctions with approximately 6\% lower bilateral trade. Aggregate diversion is weak, while normalized exposure predicts relative market-share reallocation.}

\case{Equal Profits, Unequal One-Year Persistence}{Completed empirical study}{Across 7,355 common-sample firm-years, a shift toward other profit is associated with lower next-year profit before tax ($-0.3001$) and net income ($-0.2687$). Both estimates survive Holm and wild-bootstrap inference, with explicit tail-sensitivity limits.}

\case{Vietnam Financial Disclosure Research Pipeline\projectlink{https://github.com/tthoan309-ux/financialdistress}}{Ongoing research infrastructure}{Built a resumable workflow for collecting, normalizing, validating, and versioning Vietnamese financial statements and annual reports. A temporal benchmark uses 8,292 training, 2,679 validation, and 2,710 test firm-years; accounting distress is a technical benchmark rather than the project's central claim.}

\case{Dynamic Trade Communities and Global Fragmentation}{Completed computational pipeline}{Annual BACI networks for 1996--2024 show weighted modularity rising from 0.272 to 0.416 and within-community trade from 66.5\% to 70.5\%. The package includes Leiden detection, lineage matching, robustness outputs, figures, and automated tests.}

\case{Multi-Period Delivery Routing with ALNS\projectlink{https://github.com/tthoan309-ux/h2t}}{Completed algorithmic study}{Adaptive destroy-and-repair operators, baseline comparisons, unit tests, and multi-seed tuning maintain full service on the supplied 300-customer instance while reducing the reported objective by 32.39\% relative to greedy insertion.}
